Putting mobile phone data to work for policy

The largest global initiative of its kind, helping national statistics offices, regulators and mobile operators turn anonymized mobile phone data into official statistics and better policy.

56 countries. See the map

Where the program stands

Funded by the Government of Spain and delivered with the International Telecommunication Union, the program works through two pillars: operational support to country teams, and a research program that produces the methods and open algorithms countries build on. The goal is to reach 30 countries by 2030.

  • 18 Cohort 1 countries Global window, launched November 2024. Implementation concludes in late 2026; results will be shared in 2027.
  • 6 Cohort 2 SADC grant countries And 16 trained, launched May 2025. Implementation concludes in the first half of 2027.
  • 24 Countries asking to join Beyond the cohorts, observer countries receive advisory support.
