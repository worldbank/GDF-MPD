Status briefing, October 2026

Putting mobile phone data to work for policy

The largest global initiative of its kind, helping national statistics offices, regulators and mobile operators turn anonymized mobile phone data into official statistics and better policy. Funded by the Government of Spain, with the ITU as technical partner, the program supports 18 countries in its global cohort and 6 in the SADC window, and 24 more have asked to join. The goal is 30 countries by 2030.
