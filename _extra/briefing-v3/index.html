Status briefing, October 2026

Putting mobile phone data to work for policy

The largest global initiative of its kind, helping develop country mobile phone data ecosystems that turn anonymized mobile phone data into official statistics and better policy. In partnership with the Government of Spain and delivered by the World Bank and the ITU, the program aims to durably integrate mobile phone data into the national data systems of 30 countries by 2030.
